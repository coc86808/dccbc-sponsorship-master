### Executive Summary
Adamjee Cantonment College Business Club (ACC Business Club, widely known as ABC) is the premier student-led business and leadership organization of Adamjee Cantonment College, situated in Dhaka Cantonment. Established to bridge classroom business education with real-world corporate execution, the club is celebrated for pioneering competitive business idea festivals and industrial learning tours at the higher secondary level. 

Its flagship initiatives include the **ACC Business Idea Festival** (multi-edition national and inter-college business plan and idea festival), the **ACC National Carnival Business Festival**, intra-college case competitions like **ACC Intra Exordium**, hands-on corporate industrial tours (e.g., **PRAN-RFL Factory Visit**, **Unilliance Group Industrial Tour**), and national career masterclasses. Over its active history, ACCBC has attracted corporate partnerships from leading commercial banking institutions like **Trust Bank Limited**, industrial conglomerates such as **Abdul Monem Limited (AML)**, **PRAN-RFL Group**, and **Unilliance Group**.
