### Executive Summary
The IUB E-Business Club (IUBEBC) is the flagship student business and digital enterprise organization operating under the School of Business and Entrepreneurship (SBE) at Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB). IUBEBC specializes in bridging the gap between academic business curricula and high-growth commercial realities, focusing on e-commerce, startup strategy, case solving, financial literacy, and marketing communication. 

Its flagship brand is **E-Volve**—a multi-stage business case and TVC challenge attracting top-tier universities from across Dhaka. The club has established recurring and landmark corporate partnerships with leading banking institutions such as **Eastern Bank PLC (EBL)**, brokerage firms like **Shanta Securities Limited**, corporate ventures like **Area 71 Venture Limited**, multinational FMCG giants such as **PepsiCo / Transcom Beverages**, and culinary enterprises like **Green & Pepper**.
