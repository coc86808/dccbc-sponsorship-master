## 📊 Sector-by-Sector Corporate Sponsorship Landscape

### 1. Banking & Financial Institutions (Commercial Banks & NBFIs)
*The most dominant sponsorship tier for title roles and multi-stage business case competitions.*
* **Top Targets**:
  - **Eastern Bank PLC (EBL)**: Budget specifically allocated for business and advertising competitions (*Ad Maker Bangladesh 2026* at NSU YES!, *E-Volve 2.0* at IUB EBC). Focus pitch on digital apps (EBL SkyBanking) and Gen-Z accounts.
  - **HSBC Bangladesh**: The gold standard for case competitions. Hosts annual undergraduate competitions at IBA DU (*HSBC IBA Case Competition*) and BRAC University (*HSBC-BBS Case Competition*).
  - **Trust Bank Limited**: Regular sponsor of college and university business festivals (*2nd Trust Bank ACC Business Festival*, *CreADive 2026*, *Creaventure 3.0*).
  - **Mutual Trust Bank PLC (MTB)**: High willingness to title flagship empowerment platforms (*Brainiacs 2023* at Biz Bee, *Masters of Ideation 2025* at NSU YES!).
  - **Southeast Bank PLC & NRBC Bank**: Regular co-sponsors and title sponsors for entrepreneurship competitions (*Entrepret* series).
  - **IDLC Finance Limited**: Strong appetite for data, equity, and research-focused case challenges (*ThinkTank* at BUP BCC, *Make Your Mark* at Biz Bee).
  - **Shanta Securities**: Focused on capital markets and financial literacy sessions (*Mastering Personal Finance* at IUB).

### 2. Fast Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) & Food & Beverage
*Consistently provide Title, Powered By, Refreshment, and Youth Branding packages.*
* **Top Targets**:
  - **Unilever Bangladesh** (Rexona, Lever Ayush, Sunsilk): Sponsored 5 premier clubs. Best pitched for Gift Partner, Powered By, and Case study tracks.
  - **PRAN-RFL Group** (Frooto, UP, Krazy, Potata): Sponsored 5 clubs. Provides factory tour partnerships (ACCBC), title sponsorships (VoB), and beverage/snack provisions.
  - **Fresh (Meghna Group of Industries - MGI)**: Major title budget for creative and campus festivals (*CreADive 2026* at BUP BCC, *Summer Sault 2019* at Biz Bee).
  - **Abul Khair Group** (Marks Active School, Shah Cement): High presence across school, college, and university circuits (*Marks Active School* at NSU YES!, *Shah Cement* at Biz Bee, *MIC Roadshow* at NDBC and VoB).
  - **Nestlé Bangladesh** (Nescafé, Maggi): The primary partner for coffee lounges and campus refreshments (*Ad Maker Bangladesh 2026* at NSU YES!).
  - **Perfetti Van Melle** (Center fresh, Mentos, Alpenliebe): Titled *CreADive 2020* (BUP BCC) and *Masters of Ideation 2020* (NSU YES!).
  - **Transcom Beverages / PepsiCo** (Pepsi, 7UP): Regularly partners for sports, youth carnivals, and club fests (*PepsiTurf* at IUB).

### 3. Industrial Conglomerates & Manufacturing
*Provide substantial funding for community prestige, technical excellence, and national branding.*
* **Top Targets**:
  - **Bashundhara Group** (Exercise Book, Kings, Media): Active in 4 clubs. Provided title sponsorship for *ADWiz 2.0* (Biz Bee) and sports/media coverage across NDBC, DCCBC, and JBC.
  - **Berger Paints Bangladesh Limited**: Outstanding history of title sponsoring creative advertising competitions across institutions (*ADwiz 2017* at Biz Bee, *Corporiddlerz 2021* and *CreADive 2016* at BUP BCC).
  - **Walton Hi-Tech Industries**: High willingness to support school and college entrepreneurship competitions (*Walton 3rd JED* at JBC).
  - **Abdul Monem Limited (AML)**: Titled *1st AML-ACC Business Idea Festival 2016* (ACCBC).
  - **Monno Ceramics**: Premier partner for crests, trophies, and premium branded tableware awards (*CreADive 2026*, *Ad Maker Bangladesh 2023*).
  - **X Ceramics Group**: Powered *ADWiz 2.0* (Biz Bee).

### 4. Fintech, Mobile Financial Services & Tech Startups
*Target university students for user acquisition, digital wallet onboarding, and tech adoption.*
* **Top Targets**:
  - **bKash**: Present in 4 clubs as official digital payment partner, workshop partner, and fintech conclave sponsor.
  - **Gold Kinen**: Highly active emerging fintech sponsoring entrepreneurship challenges across NSU YES! (*Entrepret Season 3*) and JBC/DUEDC (*Powerpreneur 4.0*).
  - **Shohoz & Pathao**: Consistent mobility and digital partners across business fests at NDBC and NSU YES!.
  - **Sheba Holdings**: Titled *Masters of Ideation 2025* (NSU YES!).

### 5. Media & Broadcasting Partners
*Crucial for event visibility and providing ROI to corporate sponsors.*
* **Top Targets**:
  - **The Business Standard (TBS)**: The definitive English newspaper partner across NDBC, VoB, BUP BCC, and IUB EBC.
  - **The Daily Star**: Premier publication partner for business competitions at IBA DU, NSU YES!, and BUP BCC.
  - **BanglaVision & Channel i**: The primary broadcast television channels covering opening and award ceremonies (JBC, NDBC, BUP BCC).
  - **Radio Capital FM 94.8 & Radio Shadhin 92.4 FM**: Premier FM radio coverage partners.

## 🎯 Practical Action Plan for Club R&D & Sponsorship Pitching

1. **Leverage the Established Competition Tiers**:
   - For an **Advertisement / Creative TVC Competition**: Pitch **Berger Paints Bangladesh**, **Fresh (MGI)**, **Eastern Bank PLC (EBL)**, or **Bashundhara Exercise Book**—these 4 brands have explicitly funded ad-making challenges across multiple universities.
   - For a **General 360° Business Case Competition**: Pitch **HSBC Bangladesh**, **Mutual Trust Bank (MTB)**, **Trust Bank**, or **IDLC Finance**.
   - For an **Ideation / Entrepreneurship Fest**: Pitch **Gold Kinen**, **Walton**, or **Area 71 Venture Limited**.

2. **Anchor the Pitch on Digital & Gen-Z Engagement**:
   - Banking partners (especially EBL, MTB, and bKash) prioritize on-campus SkyBanking/wallet activations, student account signups, and student ambassador networks. Include dedicated digital onboarding booths in your pitch deck.

3. **Offer Multi-Tiered Partner Packages**:
   - **Title Partner ("Presents")**: BDT 200,000 – 500,000+ (Exclusive naming rights, central stage backdrop, trophy branding, judge panel seat).
   - **Powered By / Co-Sponsor**: BDT 100,000 – 250,000 (Sub-banner branding, social media campaign, segment naming rights).
   - **In Association With / Category Partners**: BDT 50,000 – 100,000 (Beverage, Refreshment, Youth, Crest, or Food Partner).
   - **In-Kind / Media Partners**: TBS, The Daily Star, Channel i, or BanglaVision to guarantee minimum cross-channel impressions.
