### Executive Summary
Notre Dame Business Club (NDBC) is the 4th official club of Notre Dame College, Dhaka. It organizes Bangladesh's premier and largest college-level business festival, **Business Fest Bangladesh (BFB)**, along with national competitions, specialized corporate workshops, roadshows, and institutional collaborations.

## 7. Notre Dame Business Club Core Recurring Competitions & Formats
*(Extracted from NDBC Official Legacy Documentation - [Post Link](https://www.facebook.com/NDBCofficial/posts/pfbid0DVAWGescex1RUjTYHHaPNGngUyv4fQE2sYWRhwjXESXmCYyghgrP6SkykE1A58KUl))*
1. **Business Fest Bangladesh (BFB)**: The nationwide flagship business fest bringing together thousands of students across Bangladesh.
2. **Virtual Business Combat (VBC)**: Annual online competition covering Photography, Poster Design, Content Writing, Case Study, Idea Presentation, and OVC.
3. **Intra College Business Fest**: Exclusive business fest for Notre Dame College students.
4. **Hunt Down The SkillMen**: Talent hunting initiative for freshers to recruit top talent into club departments.
5. **Crack the Market**: Practical market problem-solving and marketing strategy challenge.
6. **Quizzophile**: Business quiz and general awareness tournament.
7. **Biponon & Horizon**: Official festival magazine (Biponon) and annual yearly publication (Horizon).
