# 🏆 Bangladesh Business & Career Clubs Sponsorship Intelligence Master Database

Comprehensive, verified research repository and corporate sponsorship intelligence database created for **Dhaka Commerce College Business Club (DCCBC)** Research & Development (R&D).

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Live Intelligence

- **76 Audited Student Organizations**: Comprehensive directories across Bangladesh's premier public universities, top private universities, and prestigious cantonment/model colleges.
- **172 Strictly Verified Corporate Sponsorships**: Clean, verified sponsor records with exact corporate tier attribution (Title Sponsor, Powered By, Beverage Partner, Media Partner, etc.).
- **Strict Host Attribution**: Sponsors are attributed **only** to the actual host/organizing clubs. Shared posts of other clubs' events or participant clubs are strictly excluded.
- **100% Direct Verification Proof**: Direct Facebook Event URLs or official photo/post announcement permalinks.
- **Interactive Web App**: Search, filter by sector/status, download datasets, and explore live on Vercel.
