### Executive Summary
Biz Bee is the official student business organization of BRAC University, with an established legacy spanning over two decades. The club regularly designs and executes high-profile national and international competitions including **ADwiz / ADWiz 2.0** (flagship advertisement-making competition), **Casessination / Casessination 2.0** (flagship 360° business case competition), **Brainiacs / BRAINIACS 2025** (corporate empowerment & case solving), **BIZ VERSE 2025** (inter-university strategic business battleground), **Summer Sault** (flagship club fair and youth carnival), and **The E-Carnival** (flagship entrepreneurship festival), partnering with leading multinational corporations, commercial banks, FMCG leaders, and industrial conglomerates in Bangladesh.
