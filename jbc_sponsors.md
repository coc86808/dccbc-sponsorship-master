### Executive Summary
Josephite Business Club (JBC) is the premier student-led business organization of St. Joseph Higher Secondary School, renowned for initiating high-school and college-level entrepreneurship and business competitions in Bangladesh. Its crown-jewel flagship festival is the **Josephite Entrepreneurs' Den (JED)** (spanning four national offline and virtual editions), alongside intra-school festivals like **Trailblazer 2025**, national skill-building workshops (*Road to IBA & Ethos of Digital Marketing*), and inter-club strategic partnerships (*Powerpreneur 4.0* with DUEDC, *National Talent Fest 2025* with UNYSA). JBC has secured corporate sponsorships from premier national giants such as **Walton**, **SK+F (Eskayef Pharmaceuticals)**, **Gold Kinen**, **Bio-Xin**, **BanglaVision**, and **The Financial Express**.
